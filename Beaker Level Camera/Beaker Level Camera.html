Beaker level camera

Pick the phone camera and click Start camera.

Your phone, used as a webcam, watches the reactor beaker. The page finds the water line in a narrow strip and converts it to mL from two marks you click once.

–
Not measuring.
  1. On the phone and laptop, install a webcam app (Camo, Iriun or DroidCam) so the phone shows up as a camera here.
  2. Fix the phone so it sees the beaker side-on, with the liquid line inside the view. It must not move afterwards.
  3. Click Mark A, then click a known level on the beaker (for example the 350 mL line). Do the same for Mark B at a second level.
  4. Slide the strip onto a clear part of the beaker, away from labels and tape. Clear water shows up best with a striped paper behind the beaker.
